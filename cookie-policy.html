Cookie Policy

Below is an overview of the cookies and similar technologies that may be used on NewsPulse+, including advertising and analytics services.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website or third-party service. They can help a site remember preferences, measure usage, support advertising, and improve performance.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be used to make the site work correctly, support security, load pages, process contact forms, and remember basic site preferences such as language settings.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may remember choices such as language selection or display settings so the site is easier to use on future visits.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies may help NewsPulse+ understand which pages are visited, how users move through the site, and how content performance can be improved.

Google Analytics

NewsPulse+ may use Google Analytics or similar analytics tools. Google Analytics may use cookies to collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, browser type, device type, and time spent on pages.

Advertising Cookies

Advertising cookies may be used to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent the same ad from appearing too often, and help make advertising more relevant.

Google AdSense

NewsPulse+ may use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on visits to this site and other websites.

Google Consent Mode

The cookie banner communicates your analytics and advertising choices to Google Consent Mode. By default, advertising and analytics storage are denied, and Google AdSense is not loaded until a visitor gives advertising consent.

Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services, including Google AdSense, Google Analytics, embedded tools, advertising partners, and form services, may place or read cookies according to their own policies.

Why Cookies Are Used

Cookies may be used to operate the website, improve performance, understand readership, protect forms, support translation tools, measure ads, and help fund the site through advertising.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site features, forms, language tools, analytics, or advertising behaviour.

Advertising Choices

You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings and other industry opt-out tools. Your browser may also provide privacy controls for third-party cookies.

Changes to This Policy

NewsPulse+ may update this Cookie Policy when site features, analytics, advertising tools, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page.
