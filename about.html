About NewsPulse+

News Pulse+ is an independent digital publication founded and maintained by Ivaylo Tsvetkov. The site brings together major news, politics, economics, entertainment, sport, and culture with a focus on quick summaries, useful context, and clear analysis for everyday readers.

Who We Are

News Pulse+ is run as an independent digital publishing project by Ivaylo Tsvetkov, with a focus on building a useful, easy-to-read news experience. The site is not owned by a political party, public body, or advertiser.

Our Mission

The mission of News Pulse+ is to help readers quickly understand the stories shaping public life, markets, culture, sport, and online conversation. We aim to explain what happened, why it matters, and where readers can go next.

Editorial Standards

  • Stories should be clear, fair, and written in plain language.
  • Headlines should reflect the article and avoid misleading claims.
  • News, opinion, entertainment, and viral coverage should be easy to tell apart.
  • Errors should be corrected when identified through review or reader feedback.

How Content Is Created

Stories are selected from current public-interest topics, developed into readable summaries, reviewed for clarity and basic factual consistency, and published with section navigation so readers can explore related coverage. Digital tools, including AI-assisted drafting and summary support, may help with research structure, formatting, and translation, but editorial decisions stay with News Pulse+.

What Makes Us Different

NewsPulse+ is not designed to be a simple headline feed. The site focuses on explaining the point of a story quickly, adding context around why it matters, and connecting developments across politics, economics, culture, sport, and viral attention.

Corrections & Feedback

If a reader spots an error, unclear wording, outdated information, or a source concern, they can contact News Pulse+ through the contact page. Feedback is reviewed so published pages can be improved where needed.
